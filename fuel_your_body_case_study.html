UX CASE STUDY • 2025

Fuel Your Body

Revitalizing a PEI-based meal subscription platform. We transformed a confusing one-time order process into a streamlined subscription model, increasing Average Order Value by 246% and reducing drop-off rates significantly.

Platform

Shopify Custom Build

The Challenge

Fuel Your Body is a subscription-based meal platform for busy people in PEI, Canada. We began working in January 2025 with a comprehensive audit, identifying a critical 63% drop-off rate. Users were confused by the dual "One-Time Order" vs. "Subscription" model, and the brand value wasn't clearly articulated.

Our goal was to optimize the UI/UX to highlight tiered offers (4, 6, 12 meals), clarify the value proposition, and improve the mobile experience where 75% of traffic originates.

Research Phase

Redefining the Flow

We analyzed competitors like WeCook, HelloFresh, and Factor. We noticed a pattern: successful platforms guide users through a structured funnel starting with the plan, then preferences, then payment, and finally meal selection.

Our original flow asked users to choose "Weekly vs One Time" immediately, which created decision fatigue. We pivoted to a streamlined subscription-first model.

Mobile-First "Build Your Plan"

With 75% of traffic coming from mobile, the plan selection page needed to be thumb-friendly. We introduced clear tiered pricing cards:

  • 4 Meals @ $12.99
  • 6 Meals @ $12.49 (Most Popular)
  • 12 Meals @ $11.99 (Best Value)

Data-Rich Meal Cards

Users wanted to know what they were eating without clicking through multiple pages. We upgraded the meal cards to display Calories, Carbs, Protein, and Fats instantly.

This transparency built trust and sped up the selection process, directly contributing to the increased conversion rate.
